VIDEO 2 · ~1 min 45 s

Why divide by √d_k?

A dot product is a sum of d_k products. With independent unit-variance components its variance is d_k, so dividing by √d_k restores unit variance. Includes the Monte-Carlo check and why the magnitude matters for exp.
